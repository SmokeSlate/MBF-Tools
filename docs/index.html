Features
Everything you need in one app
No separate tools, no terminal, no PC. MBF Tools handles the full setup flow on your headset.
🧭

Guided Setup Wizard

Step-by-step instructions for enabling Android developer mode, turning on Wireless Debugging, and pairing your headset — with real-time status checks at every step.

🔗

Wireless ADB Pairing

Pair and connect to ADB wirelessly from inside the app using a 6-digit code. Automatic port detection included — no manual config needed.

🎮

Integrated MBF Launcher

Starts the upstream MBF native bridge locally and loads the live MBF web app in the built-in browser. Mods load faster and stay updated from the upstream source.

🛟

Live Support Hub

Access the live FAQ and fix form from the wiki without leaving the app. Get answers to common problems while staying in setup.

⚡

Device Performance Controls

Adjust CPU, GPU, and display refresh rate over ADB with one tap. Switch between Battery, Balanced, and Max presets instantly.

📋

Debug Log Sharing

One-tap log export sends a diagnostic payload to support staff with a share code, making troubleshooting fast and accurate.

How it works
Up and running in minutes
Follow the guided wizard on your headset — no computer, no ADB setup on your end.
1

Download & install

Sideload the APK from your Quest browser or via the download button above.

2

Open the guided setup

MBF Tools walks you through enabling Android dev mode step by step.

3

Pair wirelessly

Enter the 6-digit ADB pairing code when prompted. The app detects ports automatically.

4

Launch MBF

Tap Launch MBF In This App — the mod loader opens in the built-in browser, ready to mod Beat Saber.
